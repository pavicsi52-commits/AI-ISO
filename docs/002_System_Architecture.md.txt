# SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The platform SHALL follow a modular Microservices Architecture.

Each service shall be independently deployable.

Each service shall own its own domain.

Each service shall expose REST APIs.

Each service shall support Docker.

Each service shall support Kubernetes.

Each service shall expose OpenAPI.

Each service shall expose Health Endpoints.

Each service shall expose Metrics.
